Privacy Policy — AW3STSID3STORY Artist Tool

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This application is a private tool operated by AW3STSID3STORY (the "app"). It connects to the TikTok API to post and schedule the artist's own videos to the artist's own TikTok account, and to read statistics for that account.

Data accessed: the app accesses only data belonging to the account owner's own TikTok account, under the owner's authorization. It does not collect, store, or process personal data from any other person.

Use: data is used solely to publish the artist's own content and review the performance of that content.

Sharing: no data obtained through the app is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Storage and retention: access credentials are stored securely by the operator and are never published. Account data retrieved by the app is reviewed by the operator and is not retained beyond operational use.

Revoking access: the owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and websites).
